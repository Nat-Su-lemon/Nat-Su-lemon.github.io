A UART bridge for the Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W that exposes a target MCU's serial port over either USB CDC or TCP/Wi-Fi. Uses the Linux USB gadget framework and ConfigFS to present the Pi as a CDC ACM device, with userspace C bridge programs and systemd services handling the forwarding.
